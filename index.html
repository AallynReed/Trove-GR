See Trove creatures move

Drop a creature's .blueprint parts or a .tmod, and each part lands on the attach point the game itself binds it to, ready to play every animation the rig has. Or pick a rig on the left to browse its animations. Files are read from your disk and never uploaded.

Where do the parts come from?

A mod's .tmod carries its parts. For the game's own creatures, extract Trove's archives with a modding tool, open the blueprints folder, then pick a creature under Creatures. Parts the game does not bind to a rig are listed, never guessed onto one.
